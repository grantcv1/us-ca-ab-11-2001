# California Assembly Bill 11 (2001) in Akoma Ntoso

California Assembly Bill 11, "Olive pests," by Assemblymember Dickerson, Chapter 179, Statutes of 2001, in Akoma Ntoso XML, with a CSS stylesheet. It was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini.

**This is not an official version.** For the official text, see [California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=200120020AB11).

## Why this bill

When Grant Vergottini designed CAML (California Markup Language) in 2002, this bill was his example document. CAML was his first legislative schema, and it is still in use.

## About the markup

- **Standard:** Akoma Ntoso 2.0 (AKN 3.1). The XML validates against the Akoma Ntoso 2.0 schema.
- **Document type:** This is the chaptered version, so it is an `<act name="chapteredBill">`, not a `<bill>`.
- **Digest:** The Legislative Counsel's Digest is a `<container name="legislativeCounselsDigest">` in the `<preface>`. Akoma Ntoso has no element for it.
- **Amendments:** Each amending section has a `<mod>` holding the action line and the new text. New sections are in `<quotedStructure>`. The new Part 5.5 heading is in `<quotedText>`, because a heading alone isn't a complete `<part>` in the schema.
- **Subdivisions:** California calls (a), (b) and (c) subdivisions, so they are `<subdivision>` elements.
- **Modifications:** `<activeModifications>` has one `<textualMod type="substitution">` for each of the 15 amendments, pointing from the quoted text to the place in the Food and Agricultural Code.
- **History:** `<lifecycle>` has the dates from introduction to filing.
- **Identifiers:** Every element with an `eId` also has a `GUID`.
- **Cross-references:** Citations to other law are `<ref>` elements. Their `href`s are Akoma Ntoso URIs for those laws, and no document in this archive sits at those addresses.

## Status of the text

- **Source:** The HTML of the chaptered version on California Legislative Information, read on November 4, 2026. The XML's text was compared with the source, character for character, and matched, apart from spacing.
- **History dates:** These come from the bill history as shown on Open States (Plural), because they aren't in the chaptered text.
- **What changed in the law:** The source shows the new text only, with no strikeouts or additions, so the XML has no `<ins>` or `<del>`. The text being replaced isn't included.
- **Uppercase:** The source gives the enacting clause in mixed case and displays it in capitals. The XML keeps the mixed case, and the stylesheet shows capitals.
- **Text left as it is:** The digest says "Olive, Stone, and Pome Fruit Pest Control Law," while Section 2 says "Olive, Stone, and Pome Fruit Pest District Control Law." Section 8769 uses both "assessors parcel" and "assessor’s parcel." Neither was changed.
