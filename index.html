California Assembly Bill 11 in Akoma Ntoso

Assembly Bill 11, “Olive pests,” by Assemblymember Dickerson, approved on August 12, 2001, as Chapter 179 of the Statutes of 2001. It extends California’s stone and pome fruit pest district law to olives.

View AB 11

Why this bill

When I designed CAML (California Markup Language) in 2002, my very first legislative schema, this bill was my example document. So, while this bill may not be a landmark document for anyone else, to me it was an important milestone in my life. CAML is still in use. Here is the same bill in Akoma Ntoso.

How it looks

A blue rule marks the text being put into the Food and Agricultural Code, set apart from the bill’s own words. The page is styled by CSS alone, with no script.

Status of the text

This is the chaptered version, from California Legislative Information. It shows the new text only, so the law it replaced isn’t included.

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini and validates against the Akoma Ntoso 2.0 schema. It isn’t an official version. The official text is on California Legislative Information.
